What to expect when working with us

Our Service Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Every business is different, so we don't believe in a one-size-fits-all approach. We try to understand what you need, agree on a clear plan, and keep you involved as your project moves forward. This page explains how we generally work with our clients.

1. Getting Started

It usually starts with a conversation. You tell us a little about your business, what you're looking to improve, and what you have in mind.

We'll review your requirements, ask questions if something needs clarification, and suggest a suitable approach. Not every business needs a complicated website or a large package. We aim to recommend work that makes sense for your goals and budget.

2. Understanding Your Requirements

Before starting a project, we'll discuss the important details, which may include:

  • Your business and target customers.
  • The purpose of the website or digital service.
  • Pages, features, and functionality you need.
  • Design preferences, references, and branding.
  • Budget, timeline, and delivery expectations.

Once the details are clear, we'll confirm the agreed scope and next steps before moving into the work.

3. Design and Demo Preview

Depending on the project, we may share a design concept, sample layout, demo, or preview to help you understand how the website could look and work.

A preview is meant to help us discuss ideas and direction. It does not automatically mean that every feature, page, or item shown is included in the final project. The final scope will be based on what we have agreed.

4. Development and Progress

Once the project direction and scope are approved, we begin development. This may include building pages, adding features, setting up forms, connecting databases, improving mobile layouts, and integrating agreed third-party services.

We'll aim to keep communication straightforward and share progress or previews at appropriate stages, especially when your feedback or approval is needed.

5. Client Feedback and Approvals

Your feedback is an important part of the process. When we share a preview or request approval, please try to review it and send feedback in a clear and timely manner.

If feedback or approvals take longer than expected, the project timeline may need to be adjusted. We'll discuss significant changes before proceeding.

6. Revisions and Additional Requests

We want the final work to feel right for your business. Revisions covered by the agreed scope will be handled as discussed during project planning.

New features, extra pages, major design changes, or requests outside the agreed scope may require additional time or cost. We'll let you know before taking up that work.

7. Content and Materials

Clients are generally responsible for providing the business information, text, images, logos, product details, and other materials needed for their project, unless content creation is included in the agreed services.

If you need help preparing content, finding suitable imagery, or organizing information, let us know. We can discuss whether that assistance is part of the project or an additional service.

8. Payments

The project cost, advance payment (if applicable), payment stages, and any additional charges will be discussed and agreed before work begins.

If a project is divided into milestones, each stage may require approval or payment before the next stage begins, depending on the agreed arrangement.

9. Delivery and Launch

Before delivery, we'll make reasonable efforts to review the agreed work and address issues identified during our checks.

A website launch may require access to a domain, hosting, third-party accounts, or other client-provided resources. Launch timing depends on those items being available and properly configured.

Where credentials or account ownership are involved, we'll discuss the necessary access and handover arrangements with you.

10. Maintenance and After-Delivery Support

We can provide website maintenance, updates, troubleshooting, and other support where these services have been agreed.

Unless included in your package or written agreement, ongoing maintenance, new content, additional features, third-party subscription costs, and future redesigns may be charged separately.

11. Communication

We prefer clear and respectful communication. You can contact us through the communication channels shared for your project. We'll do our best to respond within a reasonable time, depending on workload, working hours, and the nature of your request.

Urgent requests, especially those involving a live website, should be clearly explained so we can understand the issue and its priority.

12. Working With Our Team

Some projects may involve more than one person, including developers, designers, or other authorized collaborators. We may divide tasks among team members to keep the work organized and moving.

Team members will be expected to follow project requirements, respect client confidentiality, and use any access provided only for the work they are assigned.

13. Third-Party Services

Some features may depend on external platforms such as hosting providers, domain services, email systems, payment gateways, APIs, or plugins.

These services may have separate charges, terms, or technical limitations. We'll try to explain any relevant dependencies before they become part of your project.

14. If Something Changes

Sometimes requirements change, a new idea comes up, or something unexpected affects the project. If that happens, we'll discuss the available options, possible impact on cost or timeline, and how best to move forward.

15. Cancellation or Pausing a Project

If you need to pause or cancel a project, please contact us as soon as possible. We'll review the work completed, any pending payments, and the next steps for closing or resuming the project.

Any payment or refund arrangements will follow the project agreement and applicable law.

16. Our Approach

We believe good work comes from honest communication, realistic expectations, and a little flexibility from both sides. Our aim is not simply to deliver a website, but to create something useful for your business and make the experience of working together straightforward.

17. Contact Us

Have a question about our services or how a project works? Feel free to reach out. We're happy to discuss things before you decide to get started.
