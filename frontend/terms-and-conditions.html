We want every project to start with a clear understanding of what is being done, what it costs, and what both sides can expect. These terms explain the basics of working with Global Digital Agency.

Global Digital Agency provides digital services to businesses, including website development, design, branding support, website maintenance, SEO, and other services agreed upon for a particular project.

We will work according to the agreed project scope. If you request additional pages, features, integrations, design changes, or work that was not included in the original agreement, we may need to discuss additional costs and time before proceeding.

Project fees, payment milestones, advance payments, and applicable charges will be communicated and agreed upon before the work begins.

We want our clients to be comfortable with the work. Reasonable revisions included in the agreed project scope will be handled as discussed during the project.

We will make a reasonable effort to complete work within the timeline discussed with the client. Timelines may change if feedback, content, approvals, payments, or other required information are delayed.
