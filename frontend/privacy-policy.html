Your privacy matters

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

We respect your privacy and believe your information should be handled with care. This page explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used when you contact or interact with Global Digital Agency.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website or submit an enquiry, we may collect information that you choose to provide, such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Business or company name
  • Service you are interested in
  • Details or messages you share with us about your project

We may also receive basic technical information needed to operate, maintain, and protect our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and messages.
  • Understand your business needs and project requirements.
  • Prepare proposals, estimates, and service details.
  • Communicate with you about your requested services.
  • Deliver, manage, and support agreed projects.
  • Maintain website functionality and prevent misuse.
  • Improve our services and communication experience.

3. How Your Information Is Stored

Information submitted through our enquiry form may be stored in our database and used to help us manage enquiries and ongoing projects.

Enquiry details may also be sent to our designated email account to help us respond. We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure.

4. Who May Access Your Information

Access to enquiry and project information may be limited to the agency owner and authorized team members, developers, designers, contractors, or service providers who need that information to respond to enquiries or complete the requested work.

Authorized individuals are expected to handle information responsibly and not use or share it for unrelated purposes.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell your personal information. Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary, including:

  • With authorized team members working on your project.
  • With hosting, database, email, or technical service providers needed to operate our website or deliver services.
  • When required by law or a valid legal request.
  • To protect our website, business, clients, or users from fraud, misuse, or security threats.

6. Data Retention

We retain enquiry and project information for as long as it is reasonably needed to respond to you, manage a project, maintain business records, resolve disputes, or meet applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer needed, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or remove identifying details where appropriate.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have shared with us, subject to applicable law and legitimate record-keeping needs.

You may also choose not to provide certain information. However, we may not be able to respond fully to your enquiry or provide a requested service without the relevant details.

8. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use essential technical features or similar technologies needed for functionality, security, or performance. If additional analytics or tracking tools are introduced, this policy may be updated to explain their use.

9. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of websites operated by third parties. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies separately.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, or business practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, feel free to contact us.
